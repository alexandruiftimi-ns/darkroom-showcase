Things that work underneath.

    Native scroll

    The wheel is never hijacked. Every scene follows your scroll and plays back in reverse when you scroll up.

  2. 02

    Built for phones too

    Each demo has its own phone layout and lighter media, not a shrunken desktop.

  3. 03

    Calm when asked

    Visitors who turn motion off get a still, finished page. Nothing breaks without the animation.

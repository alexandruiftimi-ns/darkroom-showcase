Meet the residents

Who's moving in?

Every habitat is designed around the creature that will call it home. Here are the quick facts we tell every new keeper.

Axolotls

Ambystoma mexicanum

  • Cool water, 16–20 °C. Warm rooms stress them; a small fan helps in summer.
  • Room to roam: 75 L+. They need our Grand size, never a nano tank.
  • Fine sand or bare floor. They gulp gravel while feeding.

Cherry shrimp

Neocaridina davidi

  • Happy at 20–26 °C. A stable room temperature is plenty.
  • Busy grazers. They snack on algae and biofilm all day long.
  • No copper, ever. Check plant fertilisers and meds first.

Nerite snails

Neritina spp.

  • Algae cleaners. They polish glass and rocks without touching plants.
  • Keep the lid on. They like to go exploring outside the tank.
  • No population boom. Eggs need brackish water to hatch.

Axolotls see shrimp and snails as snacks, so they get a habitat of their own.

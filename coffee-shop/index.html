01 · Our coffee

Roasted light, so you can taste where it grew.

We buy green coffee from a handful of farms we know by name, roast it in small batches and rest it before it reaches your cup. Nothing is rushed, because nothing has to be.

  • Traceable

    Every bag names the farm, the region and the harvest.

  • Small batches

    Roasted in short runs, so every roast gets watched start to finish.

  • Brewed to order

    Espresso dialled in each morning. Filter made one cup at a time.

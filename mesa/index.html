N° 1 · Eau de Parfum

Lumière

Morning light across a linen sheet. Lumière opens bright, almost weightless, then settles into the warmth of skin like sun that has nowhere left to go.

N° 2 · Eau de Parfum

Ombre

The hour after the candles go out. Ombre is smoke and polished wood, close and unhurried, a scent that keeps its own counsel.

Top
Black pepper, cardamom, saffron
Heart
Smoked birch, leather, iris
Base
Oud, vetiver, labdanum

Le Toucher

Lumière’s jasmine laid over Ombre’s smoked woods, joined by a single drop of golden benzoin. Neither light nor shadow. The moment between.

L’Atelier · Rue de Valois, Paris

“A perfume is not worn.
It is met.”

Solène Arnaud, founder

Maison Solène was opened in a narrow workshop off the Palais-Royal, with one perfumer, one organ of raw materials and a belief that a scent should take its time. It still does. Each composition rests for twelve weeks before it is bottled, and nothing leaves the atelier until it has been worn for a full day.

We work in small batches and buy from the same growers each season: jasmine picked before dawn in Grasse, iris roots aged three years in Tuscany, oud from a single family in Laos. The flacons are blown in Normandy and finished by hand in Paris, the gold applied one line at a time.
